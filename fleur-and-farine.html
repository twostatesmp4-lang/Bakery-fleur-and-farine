Small batches, made by hand.

Fleur & Farine opened in 2014 with one oven, a marble counter and a simple rule: nothing sits on the shelf overnight. More than a decade later, we still laminate our dough by hand, ferment our bread for two days and bake in small batches all morning.

We buy our butter, eggs and fruit from nearby farms and bake only what we expect to sell that day.

  • Dough is laminated and shaped by hand.
  • Butter, eggs and fruit from nearby farms.
  • Baked in the morning, sold the same day.

A morning at the bakery

Most of our day happens before the doors open. Here is how the first half of it goes.

  1. 4:30am

    Lights on

    The ovens come up to temperature while the dough, rested overnight, is rolled out.

  2. 5:30am

    Shaping and filling

    Croissants are cut and shaped. Tarts are filled and glazed by hand.

  3. 7:00am

    Doors open

    The first croissants come out of the oven, and the coffee is on.

  4. 12:00pm

    Second bake

    Bread, cinnamon rolls and cakes for the afternoon rush.
